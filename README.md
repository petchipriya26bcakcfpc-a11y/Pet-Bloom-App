## Pet-Bloom-App



## Project Overview

 Pet Bloom is a mobile application designed for pet lovers.
 It helps users take care of their pets easily.
 The app provides pet food, accessories, grooming, and healthcare services.
 It offers a simple and user-friendly experience for pet owners.

##  Objectives

 To make pet care easy and convenient.
 To provide pet food and accessories in one place.
 To help users find pet grooming and veterinary services.
 To support healthy and happy pets.
 To save users' time through online services.

##  Design Highlights

 Attractive and colorful user interface.
 Cute pet images and icons.
 Simple navigation and clean layout.
 Easy-to-use search and category options.
 Clear product details and booking screens.
 Consistent colors and readable fonts.

##  Features

 Pet Food: Browse and purchase pet food.
 Pet Accessories: Shop for toys, collars, and other essentials.
 Pet Grooming: Book grooming services.
 Veterinary Care: Find vets and pet healthcare services.
 Pet Adoption: Explore pets available for adoption.
 Online Payment: Pay securely for orders.
 Order Tracking: Track product deliveries.
 Pet Profile: Store pet details and basic information.
